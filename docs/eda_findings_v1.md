# Initial EDA findings — electricity training period

Scope: all 7,992,493 electricity readings with reported timestamps January–August
2016, from the validated unit-standardized ETL version etl-v1-c4c56f89d676b969.
No sample was used. This is a chronological training partition, not the entire
12,060,910-row electricity collection. Validation/test target distributions were
not examined in this analysis. Earlier whole-collection quality profiling is separate.

## Zeros and missing hours

Source evidence: evidence/time_quality_20261113T150254Z-67d35722.json,
generated by scripts/check_time_quality.py on the student's VM.

- 471,058 training readings are zero. Site 0 contributes 346,055 of them, about 73%.
  At site 0, 57.32% of its 603,750 training observations are zero.
- There are 6,278 internal time-gap events affecting 965 buildings, accounting for
  206,659 missing hours between each building's observed first/last training readings.
  The longest internal gap is 2,612 hours. Unobserved period edges are not included.
- There are 10,075 contiguous zero runs; 1,466 last at least 24 hourly observations,
  affecting 336 buildings. A gap or nonzero reading breaks a zero run.
- Building 1221 (site 13) has 5,856 consecutive zero observations, covering every
  hour of the January–August training window. This is not evidence its later readings
  are zero; the analysis stops at the training boundary.
- Several site 0 buildings have matching zero runs ending May 1 at 18:00. This
  suggests a shared reporting/operational pattern, but its cause remains unverified.

Readiness implication: passing type/duplicate/nonnegative gates is insufficient to
declare every measurement reliable. Keep original values and document this risk.
Do not silently remove all zeros or all high readings. Inspect extreme readings in
building/site context before adopting a cleaning threshold.

## Historical-feature coverage

Source evidence: evidence/lag_coverage_20261113T150505Z-d605a606.json,
generated by scripts/check_lag_coverage.py on the student's VM.

| Offset | Available rows | Missing rows | Available % | Available zero readings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 hour | 7,984,805 | 7,688 | 99.90 | 469,359 |
| 24 hours | 7,933,975 | 58,518 | 99.27 | 469,098 |
| 168 hours | 7,706,058 | 286,435 | 96.42 | 465,166 |

History requires the same building and exact timestamp offset. Missing history near
January 1 is expected because the preceding December is unavailable. Zeros count
as available measurements; coverage is not a guarantee of measurement validity.

These findings justify 1/24/168-hour history plus missing indicators, calendar and
building metadata as the initial feature set. The weekly baseline is available for
96.42% of training targets; validation/test coverage will be reported independently.
Use matched eligible rows when later comparing baseline and model errors. Preserve
all feature rows initially and fit any learned missing-value treatment on training
data only. Weather alignment remains unresolved, so temperature is not yet included.

Training target-versus-lag correlations will be computed by build_features.py and
recorded in its feature-build report. They must be interpreted alongside zero-run
and missingness risks, not as proof the prediction hypothesis is met.
